Essay Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page splits and counts the words, pages and days you type. It does not say how long a piece should be, whether it is good, or what a class, editor or publisher requires.

The budget truth

A word total shared across sections by weight. Weights go in a list, separated by commas. Extra words go to the largest remainder, and the earlier section wins a tie.

The pages truth

How many pages a word count fills at your words-per-page figure.

The daily truth

Words a day still needed to reach a target.
