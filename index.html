Essay Math

The word count is the part you can plan. The words are the part you cannot.

Boundary: arithmetic only Essay Math splits and counts the words, pages and days you type. It does not say how long a piece should be, whether it is good, or what a class, editor or publisher requires. You decide that.

The budget truth

Total 1000 words over weights 2, 5, 3: 200, 500, 300 per section. The shares always add back up to the total.

The pages truth

5000 words at 275 per page: 18 full pages and 50 words, so 19 pages. A leftover word still takes a page.

The daily truth

Target 5000, written 1800, 8 days left: 3200 words to go, 400 a day (rounded up).

Run your numbers
Essay Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
